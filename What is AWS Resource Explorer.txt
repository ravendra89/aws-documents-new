What is AWS Resource Explorer?

It is A search and discovery service that lets you to find and view AWS resources across your entire account and all regions — from one place.

Problem it solves: Without it, to find all your EC2 instances, S3 buckets, Lambda functions etc., you'd have to log into each region separately. Resource Explorer indexes everything globally.

What you can do:

Search resources by type, tag, region, or name across all regions
Find untagged or orphaned resources
Discover resources you forgot about
Build views/filters for specific resource groups (e.g., "all prod resources" or "all resources in us-east-1")
